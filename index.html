01REPORTING

Rebuilding LAS reporting

I overhauled Omni’s LAS reporting workflow so engineers could generate depth-bounded reports across multiple drilling runs, choose the curves and well context they needed, include PWD and temperature data, and merge gamma-memory measurements into the final output.

I also worked heavily on performance. Reporting more than 10,000 metres of well data had taken about 25 seconds in the previous software. My implementation brought the average generation time down to roughly 6 seconds.

03FIELD WORKFLOW

Synchronization and offline field use

I worked on Omni’s synchronization with PRIME and its offline-data workflows, using API-based job and data transfer together with local persistence. Field users could download the jobs they needed, continue working without a connection, and reconcile their drilling data once connectivity was available again.

One issue I fixed involved newly downloaded jobs being stored correctly but not appearing in Configuration because the page retained old state. I changed the refresh behaviour so downloaded jobs became available immediately when the application shell reopened.

02 · EMBEDDED

STM32 Temperature
Display

I programmed an STM32 in C and wired it to a temperature sensor and an 8×8 LED matrix. I drove the display directly instead of using a dedicated matrix driver, so the firmware had to control the rows and columns itself.

The display used row scanning to light one row at a time at high speed. By updating the column values and cycling through the rows quickly enough, persistence of vision makes the separate flashes appear as one continuous image. I used that system to scroll the temperature reading across the matrix.
